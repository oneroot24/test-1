What is B.Tech credit transfer?

Continue your education without automatically starting from zero.

Academic credit transfer allows eligible students to continue their B.Tech journey by transferring credits earned from previously completed subjects or courses to another institution or eligible programme.

Is B.Tech credit transfer possible?

It may be possible, but it depends on the university, previous credits and eligibility / approval rules.

Can students with backlogs consider credit transfer?

Students with failed or pending subjects can enquire. The receiving university will determine what can be accepted and what requirements remain.

Will all my previous credits transfer?

Not necessarily. Credit mapping, syllabus matching and university rules can affect how many credits are accepted.

Which university offers credit transfer?

The right option depends on your eligibility, academic background and current university rules. Elite Education provides guidance throughout the admission process.

What documents should I keep ready?

Keep your previous B.Tech marksheets, transcripts and other relevant academic documents ready for evaluation.
